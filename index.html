💡 What This App Does

Most people who hold Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other digital assets want to answer one simple question: "If I keep doing what I am doing, where will I be in five or ten years?" hodlcalc-2 exists to answer that question.

You provide three things: an amount you are starting with, a percentage rate you expect your holdings to grow by each period, and a length of time. The app does the math and shows you a clear, readable projection. You can change any number and immediately see how the outcome shifts. This makes it a planning tool rather than a prediction tool — it shows the consequences of your own assumptions, not a forecast of the market.

Because the calculator runs entirely on your own machine, your figures stay private. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere beyond your computer.

📈 How to Read Your Results

The calculator shows a projection based on the exact numbers you enter. A few things are worth keeping in mind while you experiment:

  • Small rate changes make a big difference. Going from a 5% annual rate to a 10% annual rate does not double the final result over long periods — it multiplies it many times over. Try both and compare.
  • Time is the strongest input. Extending your time horizon by a few years often changes the outcome more than any other single setting.
  • Consistency beats size. If you add a small amount on a regular schedule, the projection grows noticeably compared with a single starting amount of the same total value.
  • Nothing is guaranteed. The output reflects your assumptions, not the market. Real crypto prices move up and down, sometimes sharply. Treat the numbers as a planning sketch, not a promise.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

  • The file will not open. Confirm that the download finished completely. A partially downloaded file will not run. Delete it and download it again from the project page.
  • Windows blocks the program. Click More info, then Run anyway. Alternatively, right-click the file, choose Properties, and tick Unblock at the bottom of the window.
  • Antivirus flags the file. Small independent programs are sometimes flagged by reputation-based scanners because they are new and rarely downloaded. You can add an exception for the folder you saved it in, or scan the file and review the result before running it.
  • The window opens but looks too small. Maximise the window using the square icon at the top right, or increase the display scaling in Windows Settings under Display.
  • The numbers look wrong. Double-check that the growth rate is entered as a percentage per year, not per month, and that the time period matches the rate you entered.
